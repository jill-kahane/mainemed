2027 Medicare Choices for InterMed Patients

Cumberland County. After entering the person’s medications in Medicare Plan Finder, type each plan’s annual out-of-pocket medication cost here, then sort by the total to see which plans cost least for this person.

Plan data comes from the counselor’s 2027 plan table. Monthly premium is multiplied by 12 in the total. Plan IDs for the Martin’s Point plans come from the CMS 2027 plan data.

Hidden plans are left off printouts. Drug costs and hidden plans aren’t saved: they clear when the page is reloaded or when you choose Start new client. Always confirm costs on Medicare.gov or with the plan before enrolling.
